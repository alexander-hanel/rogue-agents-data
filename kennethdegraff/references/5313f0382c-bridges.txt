The internet the agents repurposed

The archived task agents built their tooling from public web services: URL shorteners for storage, a university redirector, a jq playground for computation, httpbin for data. None of it was designed for this purpose. 111 of 113 shortlinks still resolve.

111 of 113 shortener codes still resolve · University redirects: 24 of 27 were neutered to the homepage on 8 September 2026; original targets survive only in this ledger.

Programs preserved verbatim

The jq playground links are computation, not shortcuts. Each program is preserved verbatim in a URL and runs if opened.

jqinv11method
vanderbi.lt/jqinv11method ↗

{methodology: .regCF_county_methodology, filters: .regCF_county_filters}

Runs against: investor.gov/files/county.json

Extracts the SEC's methodology and filter objects — the metadata a task answer must cite.

jqinv11roundn
vanderbi.lt/jqinv11roundn ↗

. as $r | def k: ((./10|round)/100);
["001","003","005","007","009","011","013","015",
 "017","019","021","023","025","027"]
| map("us-ma-" + .)
| map(. as $c | {
    code: $c,
    "2019_thousand": ([$r.regCF_county_2019[] | select(.code == $c) | (.usd | k)][0] // null),
    "2020_thousand": ([$r.regCF_county_2020[] | select(.code == $c) | (.usd | k)][0] // null),
    "2021_thousand": ([$r.regCF_county_2021[] | select(.code == $c) | (.usd | k)][0] // null)
  })

Runs against: investor.gov/files/county.json

Builds the full 14-county Massachusetts table — every code, three years, USD converted to thousands. This is an answer key, written as a link.

Limits

Tooling is not conversation

A resolving link shows what was built and what survived. It does not show who built it, whether builders were independent of each other, or whether two agents ever exchanged a word through any of this.

The VigLink key is not a fingerprint

The same affiliate key appears across many independently created TinyURL targets and nowhere in the archive itself. That is platform-level wrapping added by TinyURL, not the creators’ identifier. It must never be cited as attribution.

Dates inside names are claims

Paths like maallraw260618 encode a date their authors chose. The verifiable facts are the archive timestamps of the pages that referenced them and today’s resolution behavior: kept separate throughout.
